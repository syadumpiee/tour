ROOTED IN TRADITION

Meet the Culture of Lombok

Lombok is home to the Sasak people, whose traditions, architecture, crafts, and cultural celebrations make the island truly special.

Visit traditional villages such as Sade and learn more about the island's cultural heritage.

A TASTE OF LOMBOK

Flavours You Must Try

Make your journey even more memorable with local cuisine.

Ayam Taliwang

Grilled chicken served with a delicious spicy sauce.

Plecing Kangkung

Fresh water spinach served with spicy sambal.

Sate Rembiga

Traditional beef satay with a rich and spicy flavour.
